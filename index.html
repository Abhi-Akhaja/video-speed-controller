Privacy Policy for Video Speed Controller

Last updated: 22/11/2026

Video Speed Controller does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or browsing data.

The extension runs only when you click its icon, and only on the tab you are viewing. It changes the playback speed of the video on that page and can move the video to its end. All of this happens locally in your browser. No information leaves your device.

The extension does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

If this changes in a future version (for example, if paid features require an email address or license key), this policy will be updated before the change is released.
